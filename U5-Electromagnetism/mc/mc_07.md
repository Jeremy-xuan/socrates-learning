A teacher conducts a demonstration where a closed rectangular circuit with a light bulb is pushed at a
constant speed into or pulled away from a region of constant magnetic field, as shown. Which of the
following makes an accurate claim about the demonstration and correctly justifies the claim?
A. The bulb will be dimmer when it is pulled away from the magnetic region because the magnetic flux
will be decreasing.
B. The bulb will be dimmer when it is pushed into the magnetic region because the magnetic flux will be
increasing.
C. The work done on the circuit during either pushing into or pulling away from the magnetic region is
equal to the energy dissipated by the light bulb.
D. The current in the circuit increases when the magnetic flux increases and decreases when the magnetic
flux decreases.
E. The magnetic field does negative work on the circuit while it is being pushed into the magnetic region
and does positive work while it is being pulled away.
