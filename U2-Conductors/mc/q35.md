# Q35

The internal resistance of a battery can be determined by

(A) Measuring the open-circuit voltage
(B) Measuring the voltage under load and no-load conditions
(C) Measuring the current
(D) Measuring the resistance directly

---

**Answer: (B)**

By measuring the terminal voltage when delivering current (V_load) and the emf when no current flows (V_no-load = ε), we can find:
r = (ε - V_load)/I
