# Q6

The drift velocity of electrons in a wire is v when a current I flows. If the current is doubled, what is the new drift velocity? (Assume all other factors remain constant)

(A) v/2
(B) v
(C) 2v
(D) 4v

---

**Answer: (C)**

Drift velocity v_d is related to current by:
$$I = nAv_d q$$

For constant n, A, and q, if I doubles, v_d also doubles.

Therefore new drift velocity = 2v
