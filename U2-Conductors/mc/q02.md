# Q2

The current I in a conductor is plotted versus voltage V. Which of the following would result in a graph that is not a straight line through the origin?

(A) A metallic conductor at room temperature
(B) A semiconductor diode
(C) A good conductor
(D) A vacuum tube

---

**Answer: (B)**

A metallic conductor at room temperature obeys Ohm's law and has a linear I-V relationship. A semiconductor diode has a non-linear I-V characteristic due to the p-n junction. A vacuum tube can also show non-linear behavior depending on the operating conditions.
