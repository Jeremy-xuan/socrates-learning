# Q31

A battery with emf ε and internal resistance r is connected to an external resistor R. The current in the circuit is

(A) I = ε/R
(B) I = ε/(R + r)
(C) I = εr/R
(D) I = ε/Rr

---

**Answer: (B)**

The total resistance in the circuit is R + r (external resistance plus internal resistance).
I = ε/(R + r)
