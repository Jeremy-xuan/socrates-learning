# Q12

The resistivity of a conductor increases with temperature. Which of the following best explains this?

(A) The number of free electrons increases
(B) The number of free electrons decreases
(C) The lattice ions vibrate more, increasing electron scattering
(D) The length of the conductor increases

---

**Answer: (C)**

As temperature increases, the lattice ions vibrate more intensely. This increases electron scattering, which increases resistivity. The number of free electrons may actually increase slightly in some materials, but the dominant effect is increased scattering.
