# Q18

A 10 Ω resistor and a 20 Ω resistor are connected in parallel to a 6 V battery. What is the total current?

(A) 0.2 A
(B) 0.3 A
(C) 0.9 A
(D) 1.8 A

---

**Answer: (C)**

1/R_eq = 1/10 + 1/20 = 2/20 + 1/20 = 3/20
R_eq = 20/3 ≈ 6.67 Ω
I = V/R = 6/(20/3) = 18/20 = 0.9 A

Or: I₁ = 6/10 = 0.6 A, I₂ = 6/20 = 0.3 A
I_total = 0.6 + 0.3 = 0.9 A
