# Q5

A cylindrical wire has a length L and radius r. If the wire is stretched to twice its length, assuming its volume remains constant, what happens to the resistance?

(A) It remains the same
(B) It doubles
(C) It quadruples
(D) It becomes eight times larger

---

**Answer: (C)**

Volume V = πr²L remains constant.
When L' = 2L, then r'² = r²/2, so r' = r/√2.

Resistance R = ρL/A = ρL/(πr²)

R'/R = (L'/L) × (r/r')² = 2 × (r/(r/√2))² = 2 × 2 = 4

The resistance quadruples.
