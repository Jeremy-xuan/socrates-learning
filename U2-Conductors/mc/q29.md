# Q29

The drift velocity of electrons in a conductor is typically on the order of

(A) 10⁶ m/s
(B) 10⁴ m/s
(C) 10⁻⁴ m/s
(D) 10⁻⁸ m/s

---

**Answer: (C)**

The drift velocity of electrons in a conductor under normal conditions is very small, typically around 10⁻⁴ m/s (0.1 mm/s). This is much slower than the random thermal velocity (≈10⁵ m/s).
