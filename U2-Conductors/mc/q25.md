# Q25

The resistivity of a conductor depends on which of the following?

(A) Length
(B) Cross-sectional area
(C) Temperature
(D) Both length and cross-sectional area

---

**Answer: (C)**

Resistivity (ρ) is an intrinsic property of the material that depends on temperature. It does not depend on the physical dimensions (length or cross-sectional area) of the conductor.
