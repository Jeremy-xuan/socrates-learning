# Q26

Which of the following materials has the lowest resistivity?

(A) Copper
(B) Aluminum
(C) Silicon
(D) Glass

---

**Answer: (A)**

Copper has one of the lowest resistivities among metals (≈1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m). Aluminum has higher resistivity, while silicon and glass are semiconductors/insulators with much higher resistivity.
