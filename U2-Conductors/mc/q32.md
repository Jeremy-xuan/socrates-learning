# Q32

The terminal voltage of a battery with emf ε and internal resistance r, when delivering current I, is

(A) V = ε
(B) V = ε + Ir
(C) V = ε - Ir
(D) V = ε/r

---

**Answer: (C)**

When current flows through the battery, there is a voltage drop across the internal resistance.
V_terminal = ε - Ir

This is less than the emf when the battery is delivering current.
