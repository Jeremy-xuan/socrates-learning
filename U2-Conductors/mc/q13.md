# Q13

A metal wire has a resistance of 10 Ω at 20°C. Its temperature coefficient of resistance is 0.004/°C. What is its resistance at 70°C?

(A) 10 Ω
(B) 12 Ω
(C) 14 Ω
(D) 20 Ω

---

**Answer: (B)**

R = R₀[1 + α(T - T₀)]
R = 10[1 + 0.004(70 - 20)] = 10[1 + 0.004 × 50] = 10[1 + 0.2] = 10 × 1.2 = 12 Ω
