# Q50

Which of the following statements is true about superconductors?

(A) They have infinite resistance
(B) They have zero resistance below a critical temperature
(C) Their resistance increases with temperature
(D) Their resistance is always constant

---

**Answer: (B)**

Superconductors exhibit zero electrical resistance below a certain critical temperature (Tc). Once a current is established in a superconductor, it can flow indefinitely without any voltage drop.
