# Q1

A steady current flows through a wire. Which of the following is constant throughout the wire?

(A) Current only
(B) Current and drift velocity
(C) Current and charge carrier density
(D) Current, drift velocity, and charge carrier density

---

**Answer: (A)** 

In a steady current through a wire, the current (I) is constant throughout. However, the drift velocity can change if the cross-sectional area changes (since I = nAv_dqe), and the charge carrier density is a material property that doesn't change with position.
