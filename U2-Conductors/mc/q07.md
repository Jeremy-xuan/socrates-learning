# Q7

A wire carries a current of 1 A. How many electrons pass through a cross-section of the wire in 1 second? (e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)

(A) 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹
(B) 6.25 × 10¹⁸
(C) 1 × 10¹⁹
(D) 1.6 × 10¹⁹

---

**Answer: (B)**

Number of electrons = Q/e = It/e = (1 A × 1 s) / (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)
= 1 / (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹) = 6.25 × 10¹⁸ electrons
