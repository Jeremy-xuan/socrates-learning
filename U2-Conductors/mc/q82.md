# Q82

Which of the following materials obeys Ohm's law?

(A) All materials
(B) Metallic conductors
(C) Only semiconductors
(D) Only insulators

---

**Answer: (B)**

Ohm's law is obeyed by metallic conductors (ohmic materials) which have a linear relationship between voltage and current. Semiconductors and many electronic devices do not follow Ohm's law.
