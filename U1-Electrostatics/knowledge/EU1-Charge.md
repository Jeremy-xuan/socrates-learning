# Electric Charge

## Electric Charge (电荷)

### 1. Two Types of Charge (两种电荷)
- **Positive charge (正电荷)**: Objects that have lost electrons
- **Negative charge (负电荷)**: Objects that have gained electrons

### 2. Unit (单位)
- SI unit: **Coulomb (C)**
- Elementary charge: e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C
- Charge of electron: -e
- Charge of proton: +e

### 3. Conservation of Charge (电荷守恒)
> The total charge in an isolated system remains constant.

**Q_total(initial) = Q_total(final)**

### 4. Quantization of Charge (电荷量子化)
All charges are integer multiples of elementary charge:
> Q = n·e (n = ±1, ±2, ±3, ...)

## Charging Methods (起电方式)

### 1. Friction Charging (摩擦起电)
- Materials with different electron affinities
- Example: Glass rod (+), Rubber rod (-)

### 2. Conduction Charging (接触起电)
- Direct contact with charged object
- Same sign charge spreads

### 3. Induction Charging (感应起电)
- Without direct contact
- Uses grounding
- Opposite charge induced

## Coulomb's Law (库仑定律)

### Formula (公式)
$$F = k\frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$$

Where:
- k = 8.99 × 10⁹ N·m²/C² ≈ 9 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²
- q₁, q₂: charges (C)
- r: distance between charges (m)

### Key Points:
1. **Inverse square law** (与距离平方成反比)
2. **Vector quantity** (矢量)
3. **Action-reaction pair** (作用力与反作用力)
4. **Superposition principle** (叠加原理): Total force = vector sum

## Conductors and Insulators (导体与绝缘体)

| Property | Conductors | Insulators |
|----------|------------|------------|
| Charge movement | Free movement | Limited movement |
| Examples | Metals, graphite | Glass, rubber, plastic |
| Electron behavior | Delocalized electrons | Tightly bound electrons |

## Electric Force Calculation (计算库仑力)

**Example:**
Two charges q₁ = +2μC and q₂ = -3μC are separated by 0.05 m. Find the force.

**Solution:**
$$F = k\frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2} = (9 \times 10^9) \frac{(2 \times 10^{-6})(3 \times 10^{-5})}{(0.05)^2}$$

$$F = (9 \times 10^9) \frac{6 \times 10^{-11}}{2.5 \times 10^{-3}} = (9 \times 10^9)(2.4 \times 10^{-8})$$

$$F = 2.16 \times 10^{-1} = 0.216 \text{ N}$$

The force is **attractive** (pulling together) since charges have opposite signs.
