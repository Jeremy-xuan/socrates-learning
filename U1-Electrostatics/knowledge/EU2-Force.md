# Electric Force (库仑力)

## Coulomb's Law (库仑定律)

### Formula (公式)
$$F = k\frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$$

Or written as:
$$F = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$$

Where:
- k = 8.99 × 10⁹ N·m²/C² (Coulomb's constant)
- ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/N·m² (permittivity of free space)

### Direction (方向)
- **Like charges**: Repel (排斥)
- **Opposite charges**: Attract (吸引)
- Force acts along the line connecting the charges

---

## Superposition Principle (叠加原理)

For multiple charges, the net force on any charge is the **vector sum** of all individual forces:

$$\vec{F}_{total} = \vec{F}_{12} + \vec{F}_{13} + \vec{F}_{14} + ...$$

**Example: Three charges in a line**
```
q₁---r₁₂---q₂---r₂₃---q₃
    
    →
    F₂₁    ←  F₂₃  (if all positive)
```
Force on q₂ = Force from q₁ (pushing right) + Force from q₃ (pushing left)

---

## Vector Form of Coulomb's Law (矢量形式)

$$\vec{F}_{12} = k\frac{q_1 q_2}{r_{12}^2}\hat{r}_{12}$$

Where $\hat{r}_{12}$ is the unit vector from q₁ to q₂.

---

## Comparison with Gravitational Force (与万有引力对比)

| Property | Electric Force | Gravitational Force |
|----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Formula | F = kq₁q₂/r² | F = Gm₁m₂/r² |
| Always attractive? | No (can repel) | Yes |
| Constant | k ≈ 9×10⁹ | G ≈ 6.67×10⁻¹¹ |
| Depends on | Charge (q) | Mass (m) |

---

## Key Points (要点)

1. **Inverse square law**: Force decreases with square of distance
2. **Vector quantity**: Has both magnitude and direction
3. **Action-reaction pair**: F₁₂ = -F₂₁
4. **Superposition**: Forces add as vectors (not scalars!)
5. **Coulomb's law only applies to point charges** (or spherically symmetric charges)
