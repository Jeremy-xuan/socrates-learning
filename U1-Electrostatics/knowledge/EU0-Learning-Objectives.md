# Learning Objectives - Electrostatics

## Electric Charge
- Understand the nature of electric charge
- Apply the law of conservation of charge
- Analyze charging processes (friction, conduction, induction)
- Calculate using Coulomb's law

## Electric Force
- Apply Coulomb's law to calculate electric force between point charges
- Understand the vector nature of electric force
- Analyze superpositions of multiple charges

## Electric Field
- Understand the concept of electric field
- Calculate electric field from point charges
- Draw electric field lines
- Analyze motion of charges in electric fields

## Electric Potential
- Understand electric potential and potential difference
- Calculate electric potential from point charges
- Understand the relationship between electric field and potential

## Capacitors
- Understand the structure and function of capacitors
- Calculate capacitance
- Analyze energy storage in capacitors
