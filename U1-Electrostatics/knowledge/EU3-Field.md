# Electric Field (电场)

## Definition (定义)

Electric field is the **force per unit positive charge** at a point in space:

$$\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q_0}$$

Where:
- E: Electric field (N/C or V/m)
- F: Force on test charge (N)
- q₀: Test charge (C)

### Direction (方向)
- Direction of electric field = direction of force on **positive** test charge

---

## Electric Field from Point Charges (点电荷电场)

### Single Point Charge (单个点电荷)
$$E = k\frac{|q|}{r^2}$$

Direction: Away from positive charge, toward negative charge

### Multiple Charges (多个电荷)
$$\vec{E}_{total} = \vec{E}_1 + \vec{E}_2 + \vec{E}_3 + ...$$

---

## Electric Field Lines (电场线)

### Properties (性质)
1. **Start** from positive charges (or infinity)
2. **End** at negative charges (or infinity)
3. **Never cross** each other
4. **Density** indicates field strength
5. **Tangent** shows field direction

### Rules for Drawing (画法规则)
- Positive charge: Lines radiate outward
- Negative charge: Lines point inward
- Arrow direction = field direction
- Closer lines = stronger field

---

## Electric Field in Uniform Fields (匀强电场)

### Parallel Plate Capacitor (平行板电容器)
- Uniform field between plates
- $$E = \frac{V}{d}$$
  - V: potential difference (V)
  - d: plate separation (m)
- Field direction: from positive to negative plate

---

## Motion of Charges in Electric Fields (电场中电荷的运动)

### Acceleration in Uniform Field (匀强电场中的加速度)
For a charge q in uniform field E:
$$\vec{F} = q\vec{E} = m\vec{a}$$

$$\vec{a} = \frac{q\vec{E}}{m}$$

### Key Observations:
- Positive charge: accelerates in field direction
- Negative charge: accelerates opposite to field direction
- Acceleration depends on q/m ratio (charge-to-mass ratio)

---

## Electric Field vs Electric Force (电场与库仑力对比)

| Aspect | Electric Field (E) | Electric Force (F) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Definition | F/q₀ | kq₁q₂/r² |
| Depends on | Source charge only | Both charges |
| Exists without | Test charge | Both charges present |
| Unit | N/C or V/m | N |

---

## Key Formulas (重要公式)

1. **Field from point charge**: $E = k\frac{|q|}{r^2}$
2. **Field from multiple charges**: $\vec{E} = \sum \vec{E}_i$
3. **Force from field**: $\vec{F} = q\vec{E}$
4. **Uniform field**: $E = V/d$
