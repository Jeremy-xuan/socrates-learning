# Electric Potential (电势)

## Electric Potential Difference (电势差)

### Definition (定义)
$$V_{AB} = \frac{W_{AB}}{q} = \frac{-\Delta PE}{q} = \frac{U_A - U_B}{q}$$

Where:
- V: Potential difference (V = J/C)
- W: Work done by electric field
- U: Electric potential energy

### Key Points:
- **Work done** by electric field: W = -qEd (for uniform field)
- Potential difference is **path-independent** for conservative fields

---

## Electric Potential from Point Charges (点电荷电势)

### Formula (公式)
$$V = k\frac{q}{r}$$

For multiple charges:
$$V_{total} = V_1 + V_2 + V_3 + ...$$ (scalar sum!)

### Reference Point (参考点)
- Usually set V = 0 at infinity (r → ∞)
- For practical problems, ground or infinity as reference

---

## Relationship between E and V (电场与电势的关系)

### Uniform Field (匀强电场)
$$E = \frac{V}{d}$$

Where:
- V: potential difference
- d: distance

### Point Charge (点电荷)
$$E = -\frac{dV}{dr}$$ (gradient, direction of steepest increase)

---

## Electric Potential Energy (电势能)

### Formula (公式)
For point charge in field of another:
$$U = k\frac{q_1 q_2}{r}$$

For multiple charges:
$$U_{total} = \sum_{i<j} k\frac{q_i q_j}{r_{ij}}$$

### Conservation of Energy (能量守恒)
$$K_A + U_A = K_B + U_B$$

---

## Equipotential Lines/Surfaces (等势线/等势面)

### Properties (性质)
1. **Perpendicular** to electric field lines
2. **No work** done moving along equipotential
3. **Closer** spacing = steeper potential gradient = stronger field
4. Positive charges: highest potential near positive charge

### Visualization:
```
E field lines (→)     Equipotential lines (⊥)
    ↓↓↓                   | | |
    ↓↓↓                   | | |
    ↓↓↓                   | | |
    +++++                 ----- (positive plate)
```

---

## Key Differences (关键区别)

| Concept | Symbol | Unit | Scalar/Vector |
|---------|--------|------|---------------|
| Electric Potential | V | V (Volt) | Scalar |
| Electric Field | E | N/C or V/m | Vector |
| Potential Energy | U | J | Scalar |

---

## Key Formulas (重要公式)

1. $V = k\frac{q}{r}$ (point charge)
2. $E = V/d$ (uniform field)
3. $U = k\frac{q_1 q_2}{r}$ (two charges)
4. $W = -q\Delta V$ (work done)
