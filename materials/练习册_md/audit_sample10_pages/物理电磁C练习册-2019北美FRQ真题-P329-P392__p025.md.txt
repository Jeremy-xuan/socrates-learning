|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| For correctly substituting the current from part (b) as $I_3$                          | 1 point |

| For using a correct equation to determine the emf of the battery                     | 1 point |
| $\mathcal{E} = I_1 R_1 + V_2 = (0.051\ \text{A})(150\ \Omega) + 4.4\ \text{V} = 12.1\ \text{V}$ |         |

**Alternate Third Point**

| For calculating the equivalent resistance of the circuit and substituting this resistance into a correct equation to determine the emf of the battery | 1 point |
