Express your answers to parts (a), (b), and (c) in terms of $ \beta $, $ r $, $ a $, and physical constants, as appropriate.

**(a)** Consider a Gaussian sphere of radius $ r $ concentric with the plastic sphere. Derive an expression for the charge enclosed by the Gaussian sphere for the following regions.

$ r < a $

| For using the integral to determine the charge enclosed | 1 point |
|--------------------------------------------------------|---------|

| $ q_{\text{enc}} = \int (4\pi r'^2)(\beta r') dr' = 4\beta\pi \int_{r'=0}^{r'=r} r'^3 dr' = 4\beta\pi \left[ \frac{r'^4}{4} \right]_{r'=0}^{r'=r} $ | |

| For a correct answer | 1 point |
|----------------------|---------|
